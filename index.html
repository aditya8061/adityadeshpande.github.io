What "it depends" looks like

Two questions I hear often, and how I think about them.

"Is tech still worth getting into with AI?"

For engineers who become genuinely strong, yes. There is still real demand for deep problem-solving and systems skills. Roles built around routine work, such as basic QA, simple full-stack development and some program management, are under much more pressure. The better question is how to become very good, and whether you're willing to.

"Should I do a Master's in the US?"

With immigration getting stricter, it's much easier to justify if you have a proper TA or RA funding it. Without that, the cost and the visa uncertainty deserve a hard look before you commit, and the right program and specialization matter more than ever.
